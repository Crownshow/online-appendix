Online Appendix 2. Variable Definitions

Definitions of Variables in McNichols and Stubben (2008) Model and Modified Jones (Dechow et al., 1995) Model

2.1 McNichols and Stubben (2008)

INVCapital Expenditure (CAPEX) spent on tangible and intangible assets scaled by the sum of tangible assets and intangible assets at year beginning
CAPEXCapital Expenditure calculated as (change in tangible assets +depreciation of tangible assets) + (change in intangible assets + amortization of intangible assets)
QTobin's Q calculated at year beginning as (market value + book value of total assets - book value of equity)/ book value of total assets.
QRT2,3,
4		The indicator variables that equal to 1 if Q is in the second, third, and fourth quartile of the industry-year distribution
CFcash flow scaled by year-beginning sum of tangible assets and intangible assets
GrowthThe natural log of (current book value of total asset/one year lagged book value of total asset)

2.2 Modified Jones Model (Dechow et al., 1995) all variables scaled by total asset of the year beginning

TATotal asset
TAccTotal accrual calculated as profit from continuing operations before tax minus operating cash flow
Δ SalesChange in sales calculated as the current sales revenue minus the lagged sales revenue
Δ ARChange in account receivables calculated as the account receivable of the current year end minus the account receivable of the lagged year end
PPETangible asset
CFOOperating cash flow
